# Delivery specs demo credits

The demo opens on one programme, `on-the-sea-programme.mp4` (17.4 s, 1280x720, 25 fps, H.264 and AAC stereo), shipped as the JavaScript data pack `media/programme.js` (a page opened from `file://` cannot fetch local files). The programme was cut once with FFmpeg from the two public sources below. Each source page was opened and its licence read before use; the licence column quotes what the page states.

| Sound of `on-the-sea-programme.mp4` | On The Sea, by Ivan Turgenev, translated by Constance Garnett; LibriVox recording, reader BK (Bruce Kachuk) | LibriVox volunteers; reader Bruce Kachuk | https://archive.org/details/on_the_sea_1904.poem_librivox | "Public Domain Mark 1.0" (licence URL http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/) | 17.4 s excerpt from 22.3 s into `onthesea_turgenev_bk_128kb.mp3`, mono copied to both channels, 48 kHz, AAC; the level is as recorded (-21.1 LUFS integrated over the excerpt) |

The waves clip shows water only: no people, brands or artwork. The overlays shown in the picture (timecode, name, "FOR REVIEW ONLY") are drawn by the page, not part of any source. The programme shares its picture and speech sources with the Queue demo (`../../demo/CREDITS.md`) but is a different cut.
